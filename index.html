Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

SKILL UP is a language-learning mobile application designed to help users improve their language skills through courses, lessons, assessments, and learning progress tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains how SKILL UP collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users access the application.

By using SKILL UP, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SKILL UP may process the following information:

Account Information: Your email address, authentication details handled by Firebase Authentication, and profile information that you choose to provide.

Learning Information: Your selected courses, lesson activity, assessment results, and learning progress, where these features save such information.

Technical Information: Certain technical information may be processed by Firebase and other services used by the application to provide authentication, maintain reliability, and help prevent misuse.

SKILL UP does not intentionally request sensitive personal information unrelated to its language-learning features.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information processed by SKILL UP may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Provide access to language courses and lessons.
  • Record assessment results and learning progress.
  • Display relevant profile and learning information.
  • Maintain application functionality, security, and reliability.
  • Respond to user questions and privacy requests.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

SKILL UP uses Flutter and Dart to provide its mobile application functionality. Depending on the feature, information may be stored locally on the user's device or processed using Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, or Firebase Storage.

Firebase services are provided by Google and may process certain technical and account-related information as described in Google's applicable privacy documentation.

Information stored locally may be lost if the application is uninstalled, its data is cleared, or the device is lost. Cloud-stored information is subject to the configuration and retention practices of the relevant service.

5. Data Sharing

SKILL UP does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers, such as Google Firebase, when necessary to operate and secure the application. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

The application should not share personal information with other parties for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or required consent.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to help protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures may include secure authentication, appropriate database access controls, and encrypted connections provided by supported services.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

User information should be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's features and meet applicable legal obligations.

Users may contact the developer using the contact details below to request account deletion or the deletion of personal information associated with their account.

Requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Associated information held in connected services should also be addressed as part of the deletion process.

8. Children's Privacy

SKILL UP is intended for users who wish to learn and practice languages. The developer should establish and follow appropriate age-related safeguards if the application is made available to children.

Parents or guardians may contact the developer with questions or concerns about a child's information.
